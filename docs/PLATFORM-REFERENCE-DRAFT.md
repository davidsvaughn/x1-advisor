## What X1 is

X1 is a startup/investor platform connecting startup companies, investors,
and talent. Its core capability is AI due diligence: X1 is not a pitch-deck
summarizer — it is an AI due-diligence engine that turns company evidence
into a web-grounded, stage-aware, auditable investment memo. Startups build
profiles, upload pitch decks, and receive evaluations; investors discover
and assess companies through those evaluations, matches, and search.

## X1 evaluations

An X1 evaluation is generated by a multi-agent AI diligence workflow — not
a single model pass, and not a human analyst. The evidence (extracted pitch
deck text, the company's website content, and its platform profile data) is
sorted and handed to **seven specialist agents**, one per investor concern:
Problem Definition, Founder Background, Team Expertise, Technology & IP,
Market Opportunity, Market Conditions, and Traction & Growth. Each section
runs a research-and-critique loop: researched, reviewed for gaps (the
reviewer emits specific follow-up questions paired with web-search
queries), researched again, then scored.

The workflow is **web-grounded**: live web research is steered toward
questions where external evidence changes the answer — market sizing,
competitor landscape, verification of business claims — and the resulting
analysis carries its own source URLs. (The advisor may surface those links,
attributed to the report; they are the report's sources, not the
advisor's.)

It is also **stage-aware**: the pipeline infers the company's fundraising
stage (Pre-Seed → Growth) from deck signals, and every scoring rubric
applies stage-calibrated expectations — a pre-seed company is not
penalized for lacking Series B maturity (e.g. founder-led GTM is normal at
seed; a dedicated GTM hire is expected at Series A).

### Scoring

The overall score (stored 0–100) is not a single AI guess. It rolls up
roughly 28 sub-scores: each of the seven sections is scored along ~4 named
dimensions (e.g. Problem → Severity, Size, Validation, Solution Gap), and
sections combine through a transparent weighted formula — default weights:
Problem 30%, Team 20%, Market 20%, Market Conditions 15%, Founder 5%,
Technology 5%, Traction 5%. Section and dimension scores appear in the
report text itself; only the **overall** score and evaluation date are
stored as structured platform data.

**Critical red flags are not averaged away**: when an existential issue
(bankruptcy, shutdown, fraud, regulatory enforcement) is corroborated
across two or more sections, a hard score penalty applies and the finding
leads the executive summary with date and source.

### What one evaluation produces

- **An overall score** (0–100) and evaluation date — the structured,
  queryable part.
- **A basic evaluation report** — the standard write-up, available to
  signed-in users.
- **A premium investability report** — the full-depth memo from the same
  run; its complete text is purchase-gated per evaluation. Its existence
  and the overall score are visible to everyone; the text is not.
- **Seven section analyses** with research findings and per-dimension
  scores.

Companies can be evaluated multiple times; each evaluation is a dated
snapshot, so score history over time is meaningful. A company can also
have an evaluation on record whose report text is not in the advisor's
indexed corpus (e.g. legacy records) — the advisor can see the score but
not the text, and says so.

### What evaluations are based on — and not

Based on: the company's uploaded pitch deck (text-extracted), its public
website, its platform profile data, and targeted live web research with
cited sources.

Not based on: audited financial statements, customer or reference calls,
founder interviews, or access to private data rooms. Company-provided
claims are checked against public web evidence where possible, but an
evaluation is AI-generated diligence over available materials — not a
substitute for independent verification of material claims.

## Other platform data the advisor can see

- **Startup profiles** — company description, fundraising round/status,
  HQ, team members; draft (unpublished) profiles are visible only to
  their owners.
- **Labels** — industries (LinkedIn-style categories), target sectors
  (including company-defined custom sectors), and regions.
- **Investor–startup matches** — produced by a deterministic weighted
  rule (not AI): stage fit 30%, industry 25%, check size 20%, geography
  15%, business model 10%, yielding a 0–100 match score with per-dimension
  breakdown and reasons.
- **CVs / talent profiles** — including open-to-work status.
- **Uploaded files** — pitch decks and other documents are files on
  record; unless their text was extracted during evaluation, they are not
  searchable text.
- **Monthly metrics** — per-month operating figures a company reports
  (MRR, ARR, customers, cash in bank, monthly burn, growth rates, burn
  multiple, runway), entered by the company or synced from a connected
  source. Self-reported unless a source integration is recorded.

## Visibility rules users may notice

- Draft/unpublished profiles: owner-only.
- Premium report full text: purchase-gated per evaluation.
- Platform-hidden evaluations: not shown (admin-only).
- Private uploads: owner-only.
